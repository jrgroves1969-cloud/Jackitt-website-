CRAWLER™ Original · Design concepts under review

ONE ADJUSTABLE JACK.
THREE CONTACT OPTIONS.

Three proposed interchangeable square-top lifting inserts, each intended to address a different approved contact geometry. The enhanced-grip patterns shown in concept work remain subject to engineering review.

Flat Grip Pad

A broad, textured square contact surface proposed for suitable flat lifting points.

V-Notch Grip Pad

A shaped channel with controlled grip proposed for compatible round or angled contact points.

Step Grip Pad

A stepped contact profile proposed for compatible edges or structural lifting points.

What we're evaluating

Positive insert retention; replaceable, durable grip surfaces; secure slotted height adjustment; snap-in/snap-out wheels for positioning only; and a stable structural base with independent mechanical load support.

CRAWLER™ Original · Product concept

ONE ORIGINAL JACK.
OPTIONAL TRANSPORT CASE.

One core product. A practical accessory option.

CRAWLER™ Original is being developed as a compact, adjustable-height lifting and support system with removable positioning-only wheels, positive mechanical locking, interchangeable lifting inserts, and independent mechanical load support. Its proposed transport and storage case is an optional accessory, not a second jack or a separate product.

A closer look at JACKITT™

THE NEED. THE DIFFERENCE.
THE NEXT MILESTONE.

Our starting point is focused. Our long-term ambition is broader. Progress depends on evidence at each step.

01 / The customer

Start where the work happens.

Garages, light vehicle and trailer service users, and rental and fleet operators are the initial audiences. The first task is to identify which lifting and support challenges matter most—and what customers would pay to solve them.

02 / The proposed difference

Practical value must be earned.

CRAWLER™ Original explores a compact approach with practical positioning, straightforward operation, durability and serviceability as priorities. Comparison with existing equipment must establish where it offers a meaningful advantage.

03 / The development path

Turn the concept into evidence.

Define requirements and load cases. Complete engineering. Build a prototype. Test operation, stability and mechanical support. Assess usability and commercial fit with relevant users.

04 / The purpose of capital

Fund the next proof point.

Potential development funding would support scoped engineering, prototype fabrication, testing and refinement. Funding requirements and milestones must follow a documented scope and budget.
