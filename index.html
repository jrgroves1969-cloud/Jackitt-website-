CRAWLER™ — CURRENT DEVELOPMENT FOCUS

The first JACKITT™ development focus is a compact light-duty platform for automotive, light vehicle and trailer service applications. The final product must earn its place through verified need, sound engineering, practical operation and real-world validation.

Needs First

Start with the customer problem, operating environment and business case before locking features or scope.

Engineering First

Define load cases, boundaries, approved support points, stability requirements and mechanical support before fabrication.

Practical Operation

Simple, understandable operation with durability, serviceability and field usability kept central to the design.

Validation Before Claims

No performance, safety or market-readiness claims are treated as proven until testing and validation support them.

ONE PLATFORM. THREE DIVISIONS.

Each product family must stand on its own. Future products are not built simply to fill a lineup — each must independently justify its class.
